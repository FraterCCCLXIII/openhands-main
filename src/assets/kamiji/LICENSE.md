- The Kamiji base mesh was generated with Meshy AI (meshy.ai) as a "chubby emoji hand", then rebuilt in Blender (subdivision, finger skeleton, face rig, morph targets, animation clips). The rebuilt rig, morphs, clips and all later work are original to Paul Bloch.
